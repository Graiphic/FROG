<h1 align="center">Example 05 Numeric Compatibility Note</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Compatibility posture between the frozen Example 05 numeric surface and the standardized numeric widget baseline</strong><br/>
  <em>FROG — Free Open Graphical Language</em>
</p>

<hr/>

<h2>1. Purpose</h2>

<p>
Example 05 is a frozen executable corridor.
Its runtime-family acceptance path must not be silently broken by the broader numeric-widget modernization.
</p>

<p>
The standardized numeric baseline now uses hierarchical public members such as <code>label.text</code>, <code>caption.text</code>, <code>interaction.visible</code>, <code>interaction.enabled</code>, <code>display.*</code>, and <code>representation.*</code>.
The published Example 05 corridor may preserve a bounded flat compatibility surface for runtime acceptance.
</p>

<hr/>

<h2>3. Rule</h2>

<p>
The canonical numeric baseline should govern new numeric widget work.
The frozen Example 05 acceptance path may continue to preserve its bounded flat compatibility surface until that corridor is deliberately migrated and revalidated.
</p>
